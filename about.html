About MetCart

MetCart is a Metxon product concept for browsing devices, computing components, software and accessories through one storefront.

Product families

The catalogue separates phones, laptops, tablets, processors, operating systems, displays, batteries, cameras, chargers and accessories so each product can show specifications relevant to its own category.

Generational catalogue

Numbered product families follow a rolling-generation policy. As a new generation is introduced, older generations are retired from the active catalogue according to the configured retention window. This policy applies across supported device, accessory and component families.

Accounts and checkout

MetCart is designed to connect with Metxon account sign-in and the MetPay transaction flow. Orders and payment confirmation depend on the configured Supabase services and must be confirmed by the secure checkout response.
